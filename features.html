Features

Discover our collection of tools designed to make your daily tasks easier and more efficient. We’ve organized everything into categories so you can quickly find the right tool for the job.


  • Blind Tech Agent AI

    Meet Blind Tech Agent AI—your smart companion for answers, ideas, and everyday tasks. Chat naturally, create content with ease, and get helpful support in multiple languages, all in one place.

  • BTA Image Generator

    Create amazing images from your ideas with BTA Image Generator. Just describe what you want to see, and the AI will turn your words into a picture in seconds.

  • Age Calculator

    Find your exact age in years, months, and days with just your birth date. It's a quick and easy way to see your age instantly.

  • Calculator

    Perform basic mathematical calculations quickly and accurately. Easily solve addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division in one simple tool.

  • Currency Converter

    Check the latest exchange rate between two currencies in a simple and easy way. Enter an amount, select the currencies, and see how much your chosen amount is worth in another currency using current exchange rates.

  • Link Shortener

    Turn long web links into short, easy-to-share URLs in just a few clicks. Paste your link, choose either **Bitly** or **TinyURL**, and instantly get a shorter link that's perfect for sharing in messages, emails, or on social media.

  • WhatsApp Link Generator

    Create a direct WhatsApp chat link in seconds. Select your country code, enter your phone number, and optionally add a custom message to generate a shareable link that lets people start a chat with you instantly even without saving your number.

  • Telegram Link Generator

    Create a direct Telegram link that makes it easy for people to connect with you. Enter your Telegram username to generate a shareable link that opens your profile or chat with a single click.

  • Text Analyser

    Analyse and Review your text with helpful details in seconds. Enter or paste your text to quickly see the word count, character count, and other useful information to better understand your content.

  • Text Encrypter & Decrypter

    Convert text into secret code to keep messages private, and convert them back to readable text.

  • Text to Image

    Save your text as an image file and customize the colors and fonts to look it stylish.

  • Text OCR

    Extract readable text from images, photos, or scanned documents.

  • PDF Reader

    Read your PDF documents in a simple and comfortable way. Listen with text-to-speech, move through pages easily, ask AI questions about what you're reading, and use helpful tools to make reading more convenient.

  • PDF Merge

    Combine two or more PDF files into a single document in just a few clicks. Arrange your files and create one organized PDF with ease.

  • PDF Split

    Extract specific pages or a range of pages from a PDF into a new document. Keep only the pages you need without changing the original file.

  • PDF Compress

    Reduce the size of your PDF file while keeping it easy to read. Smaller files are quicker to share, upload, and store.

  • Image to PDF

    Turn one or more images into a single PDF document in just a few steps. It's a simple way to organize, share, or save your images as one file.

  • Document Creator

    Create professional documents quickly and easily. Type or use voice input to add your content, choose your preferred format, and download your document as a Word, PDF, or text file. You can also keep track of your writing with live character, word, and line counts.

  • Media Player

    Play your audio and video files with a simple, accessible media player. Control playback, adjust the speed, rewind or skip forward, view file details, and enjoy a smooth listening or viewing experience with easy keyboard shortcuts.

  • Password Generator

    Create strong, secure passwords and keep them organized in one place. Customize your passwords with letters, numbers, and symbols, then save them for easy access whenever you need them.

  • Speaker Cleaner

    Help remove dust and moisture from your mobile speaker using specially designed cleaning sound frequencies. Choose a frequency and start the cleaning process to help improve your speaker's performance.

  • Stop Watch

    Measure time accurately with a simple and easy-to-use stopwatch. Start, pause, resume, and reset the timer whenever you need to track your activities.

  • QR Generator

    Create custom QR codes for text, websites, phone numbers, and popular platforms like WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, and YouTube. Choose your colors, generate your QR code instantly, then download or copy it for easy sharing.

  • To-Do List

    Stay organized by creating and managing your daily tasks in one place. Add new tasks, keep track of what you need to do, remove completed items, or clear your entire list whenever you're ready.

We hope these tools make your daily digital life a bit easier.
