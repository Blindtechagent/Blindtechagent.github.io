PDF Reader

Welcome to our PDF Reader tool! This tool lets you open and read PDF documents quickly and easily. Simply select a PDF file from your device, and the text is extracted almost instantly, so you can start reading right away without waiting for processing.

You can read the document page by page, move to the next or previous page, jump directly to any page, and listen to the text using the built-in text-to-speech reader with adjustable reading speed.

You can also copy text from the current page, save the current page or the entire document as a text file, and ask AI questions about the page you're reading to get explanations, summaries, or answers.

Enjoy a fast, simple, and accessible PDF reading experience on your mobile device, computer, or laptop.
